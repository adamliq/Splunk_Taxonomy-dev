Splunk Object Taxonomy

Every kind of object that exists inside a Splunk deployment — knowledge objects created inside apps (reports, alerts, macros, field extractions, lookups...) and platform/admin objects that define the deployment itself (indexes, roles, clusters, forwarders...) — plus SOAR and UEBA, adjacent products that integrate with Splunk rather than living inside its own config-file architecture. Organised into 10 domains with config file, permission scope and definition for each.
